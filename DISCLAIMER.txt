1. WHAT THE DRIVER DOES

The driver is a pass-through. It sends to the vehicle whatever the
J2534 application you run tells it to send, and hands back what the
vehicle answers. It does not judge whether a message is safe, and
neither does the WiCAN: diagnostic requests, actuator tests, coding
changes and control unit reprogramming all go to the vehicle as the
application sends them. Do not rely on the driver or on the WiCAN to
stop a harmful command.


2. THE RISKS

Using the driver can, among other things:

  * permanently damage ("brick") a control unit. This is most
    likely when programming is interrupted or goes wrong: a WiFi or
    USB link that drops, a PC that sleeps or crashes, a weak
    vehicle battery, a wrong or corrupt file, or an error in the
    driver, in the WiCAN firmware or in the application;
  * leave the vehicle unable to start or drive until a dealer or a
    specialist repairs it;
  * erase or corrupt keys, immobiliser data, coding, adaptations or
    calibration data;
  * make parts of the vehicle operate without warning, or switch
    off systems the vehicle needs to be safe;
  * cause damage to property, injury or death;
  * void the vehicle's warranty or insurance cover, or make the
    vehicle illegal to use on the road.
